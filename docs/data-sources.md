# Data sources

Optional connectors are disabled by default: user-provided APIs, documented free GDELT endpoints, official SEC EDGAR, supplied RSS/Atom feeds, and authorized Naver Search/DataLab APIs. For Korean conversation research, read [Korean source strategy](korean-source-strategy.md). Arbitrary HTML scraping, Google Trends, paywall circumvention, anti-bot bypasses, and unauthorized APIs are prohibited. Connector status and freshness are surfaced without revealing secrets.

The optional Korea MOIS press-release connector is separate from publisher and community feeds. It uses the ministry's documented RSS endpoint without a key; it checks each recent linked article for the MOIS KOGL Type 1 mark and persists only title, original URL, publication time, and license metadata. It provides official-policy context, not market conversation or sentiment. Keep it disabled unless explicitly enabled through `MOIS_PRESS_RELEASES_ENABLED`; see the per-source terms and primary citations in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
# Current production status

Production audit on 2026-10-06 passed 31/34 checks. The three open gates were configured private-account sign-in (GitHub OAuth is disabled), at least 20 actual conversation records in the prior 24 hours (13 were present), and at least one evidence-qualified lead (none existed). Those 13 were licensed forum records from just two operators, Typst and Fedora; the audit counted zero Stack Exchange, Mastodon, Lemmy, or Bluesky records in that 24-hour database window. Of 1,047 recent records, 1,023 were metadata-only Wikimedia revisions and do not count as conversation. The same audit found four reporting/RSS/GDELT-index records, three licensed-analysis records, and four official-policy records; these are separate evidence classes. The configured discussion connectors are operational, but the stored conversation sample remains narrow and is not evidence of representative international discourse. The Stack Exchange collector was updated on 2026-10-06 to add a Politics election title query; its effect on stored records awaits scheduled ingestion, and one API probe result outside the 24-hour window does not qualify as a lead.

The scheduled production pipeline is configured for individually licensed Stack Exchange questions, Typst Forum and Fedora Discussion topic metadata, The Conversation analysis metadata, Global Voices reporting metadata, European Commission official context, and Wikimedia talk-page metadata. Stack Exchange retains only individually CC BY-SA 4.0 questions with attribution and no bodies; it is expert Q&A, not general social conversation or representative public opinion. Typst Forum retains only CC BY 4.0 topic titles and attribution after its explicit license effective cutoff; it covers a narrow software-typesetting community, not financial or population sentiment. Fedora Discussion retains unchanged topic titles, original-poster attribution, latest-activity date, and a reply-count snapshot under its default CC BY-SA 4.0 terms; its latest endpoint is capped at 30 topics and represents one selected Linux community, not a broad public sample. Lead review compares exact same-language two-to-four-word Fedora topic-title phrases to licensed headlines; matches are research cues only, not generated theses. Explore lets visitors search up to four Stack Exchange communities using a separate phrase per community. The optional Mastodon viewer supports a distinct transient hashtag per selected server across four fixed server views; exact server/tag pairs remain visible with results, and no post content or derived metrics are stored. Mastodon posts remain author-owned and no blanket content license is implied. Explore also offers transient Lemmy searches across lemmy.world, discuss.tchncs.de, feddit.org, and feddit.uk. These are separate, incomplete federation indexes, not independent audience samples or country proxies. See the per-instance terms, privacy, retention, and non-reuse caveats below. GDELT scheduled ingestion is disabled after repeated HTTP 429 responses; on-demand search may also be throttled. Historical Google News redirects are excluded and hidden; Hacker News is withheld pending rights review.

## Connector access and operating constraints (rights review 2026-09-30; activity checked 2026-10-06)

- **Typst Forum RSS:** The forum's current public guidelines license all posts under CC BY 4.0 and name author attribution as a condition. The forum's license-change thread gives the earlier effective cutoff; ingestion therefore accepts only topic posts dated on or after `2024-09-15T13:33:00Z`. One daily request is made to the explicitly published `latest.rss` feed. Only the unchanged topic title, RSS author, date, and original topic link are stored; descriptions and post bodies are discarded. The current activity window is capped by the feed's 30-topic limit. This is one narrow software-typesetting community operated by the Typst team, not finance discussion or a measure of broad public opinion. Language and geography are not inferred. No account, API key, fee, or terms acceptance is used. [Forum guidelines](https://forum.typst.app/guidelines), [license effective-cutoff discussion](https://forum.typst.app/t/licence-for-posts-and-comments-on-the-forums/137), [public RSS feed](https://forum.typst.app/latest.rss).

- **Fedora Discussion:** Its [site terms](https://discussion.fedoraproject.org/tos) say all contributions must use a Fedora-acceptable license and default contributions without another notice to CC BY-SA 4.0. The public Discourse [`/latest.json` endpoint](https://discussion.fedoraproject.org/latest.json) returned HTTP 200 and 30 topics in a read-only probe on 2026-10-01. One daily request reads this bounded latest-topic sample. Only the unchanged topic title, original-poster username for attribution, link, last-activity timestamp, and reply-count snapshot are stored; post bodies, replies, topic tags, profile names, and other profile details are discarded. The activity window uses `last_posted_at`, while the linked topic can be older; this is not a new-topic count. This is a selected English-language Fedora/Linux community, not finance discussion, broad retail-investor word of mouth, or a proxy for contributor geography or population opinion. The current response contains only 30 topics, so it is incomplete. No account, API key, fee, or click-through rights acceptance is used. [Fedora Discussion terms](https://discussion.fedoraproject.org/tos), [CC BY-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [Discourse API documentation](https://docs.discourse.org/).

- **RSS/Atom:** Each publisher's terms govern access, excerpts, retention, and republication. The configured Google News redirect feeds were not direct publisher feeds and are rejected; there are currently no direct feeds enabled. Store source links and minimal excerpts only after source-specific use is reviewed. No blanket republication right is inferred from RSS availability.
- **Hacker News Search API:** The documented Algolia-backed API exposes public item/comment search and documents a 10,000-request-per-hour per-IP limit. Public API access does not itself establish reuse/display rights for user comments. Ingestion and display are disabled pending rights clearance. [API documentation](https://hn.algolia.com/api).
- **SEC EDGAR:** Official submissions/XBRL endpoints are public, but SEC asks automated clients to identify themselves with a descriptive User-Agent and caps access at 10 requests/second. Current SEC_USER_AGENT is not configured, so the connector is not enabled. Do not invent or transmit a personal contact address. [SEC fair-access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions).
- **OpenDART:** The official API is generally free but requires account registration and a 40-character authentication key; quotas can change and are published by the operator. It is not an anonymous no-key source and is not enabled. No account was created and no key requested. [OpenDART usage terms](https://opendart.fss.or.kr/intro/terms.do), [developer guide](https://opendart.fss.or.kr/guide/main.do).
- **GDELT:** The project states its datasets are available without fee and may be redistributed with citation/link attribution. The deployed connector is limited to one global query/day and observes the endpoint's five-second guidance on 429, but recent production runs still fail with HTTP 429. Do not repeatedly probe or evade throttling. [Terms](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html), [DOC API overview](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/).
- **Stack Exchange API:** Keyless searches cover English Economics, Money, AI, Data Science, Security, Politics, and Law plus Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, and Russian Stack Overflow. One daily run makes 34 title-only queries, including the new Politics `election` query. Only explicit `CC BY-SA 4.0` items with attribution are retained; bodies and other-licensed items are discarded. The latest 24-hour audit found no Stack Exchange conversation records, so the connector's success status must not be mistaken for timely or broad conversation coverage. Observed exact tags on repeated questions are descriptive sample observations, not trends or leads. [Search API](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/search-advanced), [API throttles](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/throttle), [content licensing](https://stackoverflow.com/help/licensing).
- **Live topic exploration:** A visitor can search up to four communities using one keyless Stack Exchange request per community and a separately supplied 3–80 character title phrase for each selected language. Only explicitly CC BY-SA 4.0 items with author attribution are rendered. Queries and results remain in the browser and are not persisted by Signal Scout; Stack Exchange receives each request directly. Community panels stay separate; multiple Stack Exchange communities are still one source operator and the Q&A sample does not measure general discussion prevalence or create a qualified thesis lead. [Search API](https://api.stackexchange.com/docs/search-advanced), [content licensing](https://stackoverflow.com/help/licensing).
- **Mastodon public hashtag timeline:** The official API allows an unauthenticated public hashtag request when the selected instance has public preview enabled; the instance may deny it, in which case the app does not log in or retry through another route. The viewer supports up to four fixed servers and lets the visitor supply a separate hashtag to each; the exact host/tag pair is preserved in the transient view and brief citation context. Each request asks for at most 20 public statuses, filters out non-public/boosted items, displays author/origin server and canonical links, suppresses post bodies when a content warning exists, and keeps returned data in browser memory only. mastodon.social’s effective terms (2026-08-31) state authors retain ownership and license Mastodon GmbH only to operate/maintain the service; therefore Signal Scout does not imply a blanket redistribution license or build a persistent/derived dataset from these posts. Other selected instances have their own terms; a visitor should review them. This is a narrow interactive public client, not an authorized social-listening archive or complete fediverse view. [Hashtag timeline API](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/timelines/#view-hashtag-timeline), [Terms of Service API](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/instance/).
- **Global Voices RSS:** Enabled as a daily twelve-edition international reporting feed (English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Italian, Dutch, Yoruba, Ukrainian, Greek, Catalan). The six latest additions were included only after live checks confirmed exact publisher-channel identity and recent items with author bylines and first-party links. Signal Scout verifies the publisher's CC BY default and rejects conflicting item-level rights, then retains unmodified edition headlines, bylines, dates, first-party links, language-edition labels, and limited categories for a rolling seven-day publication window. Descriptions, body text, and media are discarded. Localized editions may be translations from the same publisher and are not independent corroboration. This is editorial reporting, not general forum conversation or a representative measure of public opinion. [Republishing policy](https://globalvoices.org/about/global-voices-attribution-policy/), [RSS directory](https://globalvoices.org/feeds/).
- **Lemmy public forum search:** The official Lemmy API documentation describes the API for app/client developers. Explore sends a visitor-entered query directly to any selected fixed host among `lemmy.world/api/v3/search`, `discuss.tchncs.de/api/v3/search`, `feddit.org/api/v3/search`, and `feddit.uk/api/v3/search`; each host receives a separate request and has a 20-result cap. Results are limited to recent, public post title, author, community, language ID, date, and an HTTPS original link. Deleted, removed, NSFW, bot-marked, stale, future-dated, and unlinked items are filtered. Post bodies are received only as part of the provider response and discarded before app state; nothing is retained in Signal Scout storage or analytics. The visitor must read the legal/privacy links for each selected instance and affirm before searching. feddit.org describes a German/English-speaking community and links its first-party data-protection policy; feddit.uk describes itself as UK-centric, publishes legal/privacy information at `/legal`, and requires users to be over 18. Public access does not grant a blanket content license, so no post body or derived metric is republished. Results can duplicate federated posts and are not independent corroboration; request limits, instance moderation, language, federation, and indexing affect coverage. [Official API overview](https://join-lemmy.org/docs/contributors/04-api.html), [Lemmy.World terms](https://legal.lemmy.world/tos/), [discuss.tchncs.de legal/privacy page](https://discuss.tchncs.de/legal), [tchncs privacy information](https://tchncs.de/privacy), [feddit.org legal/privacy](https://feddit.org/legal), [feddit.org privacy policy](https://wiki.fediverse.foundation/books/fediverse-foundation-announcements/page/data-protection-policy), [feddit.uk legal and privacy information](https://feddit.uk/legal).
- **Bluesky:** Visitor-triggered, unauthenticated public search is used only for transient source links and citation metadata. Post text is discarded by the browser workflow and is not stored. This is an incomplete, query-selected sample, not population-level conversation measurement. Scheduled ingestion and persistence remain disabled. [Official API reference](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-search-posts).

These are implementation notes, not a legal opinion. Recheck source terms before enabling a connector or displaying more than minimal excerpts.

Bluesky is available only for visitor-triggered, citation-only searches; no Korean general forum API/source is currently active. SEC EDGAR is not part of the current homepage evidence flow. Configuration, successful fetches, and permission to display full source text are separate questions; respect publisher/API terms and prefer linking out.
