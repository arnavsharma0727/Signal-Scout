# Limitations

Atlas searches selected public indexes; it does not comprehensively scrape the web or provide a representative global sample. Coverage depends on user-entered phrases, provider ranking/indexing, languages, source activity, API limits, date windows, and current availability. One provider may return partial results while another fails or is intentionally not queried; source status is displayed separately.

Lexical matching cannot reliably resolve every ticker, alias, common word, translation, spelling variant, or paraphrase. Alternate phrases must be entered explicitly. Headline-only sources can omit body-only matches; short excerpts do not replace reading the source in context. Syndicated or repeated claims may appear across sources and are not automatically deduplicated semantically.

Language, platform, publisher, server, or community does not establish a contributor's location. Returned records are not unique people, population sentiment, investor attention, prevalence, or market behavior. Online conversation alone does not establish a company's demand, margins, financial materiality, causality, or valuation. Atlas does not issue investment recommendations.

The current overview is an evidence-linked sample of excerpts/headlines, not a generated synthesis. Treat it as a discovery aid and verify consequential claims against original and primary sources. Search-result pages do not save user query history; separate operational ingestion may retain historical metadata under its own controls.
