# Source handling

Production ingestion stores records returned by individually enabled source connectors. Available metadata may include source name/domain, URL, publication time, market/language labels, title, and connector metadata. Metadata completeness depends on the source; source-specific content is filtered according to its reviewed retention and attribution rules.

Stack Exchange Economics, Money, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Information Security plus Spanish-, Portuguese-, Japanese-, and Russian-language Stack Overflow are separate international expert Q&A communities. They are not general-purpose forum samples. The connector stores only per-item CC BY-SA 4.0 results with author, source, language, tags, and license attribution, and discards post bodies. Production source health reports successful multilingual scheduled runs. The API query selection and one-page-per-term limit mean records are incomplete. GDELT scheduled ingestion is disabled after repeated HTTP 429 responses; on-demand requests may also be throttled. Bluesky is disabled following HTTP 403 responses. No Korean forum connector is active.

The currently enabled licensed analysis/reporting feeds include The Conversation's English editions and Global Voices' English feed, alongside separately labeled European Commission official context. These publication feeds are not general forum conversation. Individual rights checks and source attribution govern storage; story bodies/summaries are discarded where the connector requires title-only retention. They should not be counted as independent publishers solely because multiple editions or feed items appear.

Lead review's discussion–reporting overlap is computed transiently from the preceding seven days of eligible Stack Exchange questions and licensed analysis/reporting rows. An overlap is shown only when a licensed question carries an exact tag that also appears in its title, and the same normalized phrase appears at token boundaries in a same-language licensed headline. The view returns linked original titles/dates/source labels, caps evidence links per phrase, and does not merge translations or paraphrases. It is lexical discovery—not proof of shared event, influence, source independence, sentiment, financial materiality, or a lead. No extra content is stored by this calculation.

On-demand Explore also searches one selected Wikinews language edition for main-namespace article titles updated within 30 days. It requests no snippets or article text and checks each edition's live MediaWiki `rightsinfo`; editions without a CC BY or CC BY-SA license URL are rejected. Results are transient and linked to the original item with its license. Wikinews is community-written news, not a discussion forum or an independent operator from Wikipedia/Wikimedia; edition language is not contributor or audience geography, and its recent result volume may be sparse. It does not create or support a lead by itself.

Do not infer source quality, permission to republish, or population representativeness solely from successful retrieval. Preserve attribution and links, minimize copied text, respect source terms and rate limits, and disable a source if the intended use is not permitted.
