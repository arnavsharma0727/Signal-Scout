# Cross-Market Product Summary

Signal Scout now presents itself as a cross-market narrative-topic divergence tool. The core implemented math computes source-tier/entity-adjusted weighted topic shares, Beta/Laplace smoothing, local-minus-English Narrative Topic Difference, optional log-odds, evidence thresholds, recency decay, local independent-source concentration, and a transparent Cross-Market Research Priority. The public UI remains data-free until real profiles and authorized sources are configured.
